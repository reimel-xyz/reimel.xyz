Shifts+

Shift log · Pay tracker · Overtime & night premiums

Every shift you work, with its breaks, overtime and premiums, and the pay it should produce, worked out in front of you. When a payslip looks wrong, you are not arguing from memory: you have the record.

Privacy policy

Your hours, and the pay they should produce

Log a shift in seconds

Tap to start and stop a live shift, or enter times after the event. Breaks, multiple jobs with their own rates, and quick templates for the shifts you work again and again.

The calculation, shown

Every pay figure opens into the working behind it: which hours were regular, which were overtime, which fell in a night premium window, and what each was paid at. Nothing is a black box.

Overtime and premiums

Weekly overtime after your threshold, night and weekend premium windows, and day-rate shifts all handled by rules you set once per job.

Holiday pay

Book time off and see the holiday pay it should carry, calculated from your own recent weeks the way UK holiday pay works.

Weeks, months and trends

A calendar of everything you have worked, weekly and monthly summaries, and a trend strip so a quiet month is visible before payday makes it obvious.

Offline and private

No account and no cloud: your shifts, rates and pay stay on your device. Backups go to a folder you choose. No analytics, no tracking, no ads.

Shifts+ keeps your own record of hours and pay. Figures are estimates built from the rules you enter: a record to check a payslip against, not payroll software or legal advice.
