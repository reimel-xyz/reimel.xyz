Mirath+

Faraid calculator · Hanafi · Reasoning shown

Islamic inheritance shares under the Hanafi madhhab, with the reasoning behind every share, not just the final number. Enter the estate and the surviving heirs, and each person’s exact fraction, percentage and amount is worked out with the rule that produced it.

Privacy policy

Every share, with its reasoning

The reasoning, per heir

Tap any heir and see why they receive what they do: the fixed share or residue rule applied, and who blocked whom. The answer you can explain is worth more than the answer alone.

A family tree of the estate

The whole household drawn as a tree, generation by generation, each heir with their shares and amount, and blocked relatives shown as blocked rather than silently missing.

The hard cases handled

Fixed shares, residue (asaba), and the classical difficulties: awl, radd, the umariyyatan, mushtaraka and the Hanafi rulings on blocking (hajb). Built specifically for the Hanafi school.

It refuses to guess

A case the app is not built to answer, such as distant kindred, is referred to a scholar in plain words instead of a made-up number. Some results also carry a notice to confirm with a mufti.

A PDF for your scholar or solicitor

Export a formal document: the estate worksheet, the distribution table, the family tree and the full reasoning, ready to hand to a scholar, a solicitor or the family.

Offline and private

No account and no cloud: the estate, the names and the shares stay on your device. No analytics, no tracking, no ads.

Mirath+ is an aid to understanding and estimating faraid shares. It is not a fatwa and does not replace a qualified scholar. For the distribution of a real estate, please confirm the result with a knowledgeable scholar, and take legal advice about the law that applies to the estate.
