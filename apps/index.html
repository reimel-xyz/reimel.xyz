Qard

Mirath+

An Islamic inheritance (faraid) calculator for the Hanafi madhhab. Enter the estate and heirs and see each share with the reasoning behind it, a family tree of the household, and a PDF you can take to a scholar or solicitor. Fully offline; nothing leaves your device.

An aid, not a fatwa. Confirm a real distribution with a qualified scholar.
