Tasbih+

Tasbih counter · Prayer times · Qibla · Quran

A complete Islamic companion in one free app: a dhikr counter, accurate offline prayer times, Qibla direction, a Quran reader and daily adhkar. No ads, no account, no tracking.

Privacy policy

Everything you need, fully offline

Tasbih & dhikr counter

A clean digital tasbih with targets, rounds and vibration feedback. Count subhanallah, alhamdulillah, allahu akbar or your own dhikr, and keep your daily totals.

Prayer times & reminders

Accurate prayer times calculated on your device, with adhan and prayer notifications. Your location never leaves your phone.

Qibla compass

Find the Qibla direction anywhere, instantly, using your phone’s compass. Works without a connection.

Quran reader & khatm plans

Read the Quran offline and follow a juz-based khatm reading plan that advances as you read.

Adhkar & daily worship

Guided morning and evening adhkar, plus a prayer and fasting tracker to keep your daily worship on track.

Free forever

Tasbih+ is completely free with every feature included. No ads, no paywall; just an optional voluntary tip if you’d like to support development.
