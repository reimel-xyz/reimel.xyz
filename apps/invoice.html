Invoice+

Invoices · Quotes · The work behind every line

Log the time, the expenses and the miles as you go, and the invoice is already written: every line priced, and every figure on the PDF exactly what it says on your screen. For freelancers, trades, cleaners, consultants, anyone who bills their own clients.

Privacy policy

From the work to the invoice, in one tap

Start the timer, or log it after

Stop the timer and the entry is waiting under Unbilled at that client's rate. Or enter it later as hours, a start and end time, a flat price for the job, or a quantity at a price per unit: ten vans at twenty each.

Expenses and mileage in the same log

At your own rate per mile, appearing on the invoice as their own lines, so what you spent getting there is billed rather than forgotten.

Nothing is added up twice

Everything unbilled for a client is one tap away when you draft, and the draft's total is the sum of what you put on it. Void an invoice and the work goes back to unbilled.

Quotes that become invoices

Send a quote with its own number and a valid-until date. When the client says yes, convert it: the invoice draft arrives with the same lines and the work behind them, and the quote keeps its record.

A PDF that looks like yours

Three styles, thirteen brand colours and a live preview of your own invoice as you choose. Your logo, your signature drawn once, your payment details and terms at the foot, your tax number where a client looks for it.

Tax and rounding done properly

Type the tax you actually charge, or leave it at zero. Prices include tax or exclude it, unit prices carry four decimals, and every line is rounded once, so fifteen items come to exactly what you meant.

What is owed, at a glance

Home shows what is unbilled and what is unpaid with the overdue first, and a home-screen widget carries the same figures with the running timer.

Offline and private

No account and no cloud. Your clients, work and invoices stay on your phone and are never sent anywhere. No analytics, no tracking, no ads.

Invoice+ produces documents from the figures you enter. It is not accounting or tax advice, it files nothing with HMRC, and it is not affiliated with HMRC. For anything that matters, check with an accountant.
