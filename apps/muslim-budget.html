Muslim Budget

Budgeting · Net worth · Zakat calculator

A budgeting and wealth tracker built for Muslims: track your spending, accounts and net worth offline, with a real zakat engine that watches your hawl and tells you exactly when zakat is due and how much.

Privacy policy

Your money and your zakat, handled properly

A real zakat calculator

Not a one-off form: Muslim Budget tracks your zakatable wealth over the hawl, applies the nisab and tells you when zakat becomes due and the exact amount, with a history of what you’ve paid.

Offline budgeting

Track income, expenses and budgets by category, fully offline. Your financial data never leaves your device.

Accounts & net worth

Bring your cash, bank accounts, savings and holdings together and watch your net worth, with multi-currency support.

Loans, the halal way

Record money you’ve lent and borrowed and keep interest-free lending organised, including how loans affect your zakat.

Private by design

No account, no cloud, no analytics. The only optional network use is fetching live metal prices for the nisab, and you can enter them manually instead.

One-time Pro unlock

The essentials are free, and Pro is a single one-off purchase. No subscriptions.
