Inventory+

Home contents · Tenancy condition · Reports you can send

Two records that are easy to wish you had and hard to make after the fact: what you own, and what condition a rented property was in. Photograph it once, and the evidence already exists when you need it.

Privacy policy

What you own, and what condition it was in

Your home, room by room

Photograph each item, note what it cost and what it would cost to replace, add the serial number and keep the receipt. When you need to claim, the record already exists, with a total that adds up.

A room arrives with its checklist

Add a kitchen and the white goods are already listed; add a garden and it brings fences and bins. A check-in is never a blank page you have to invent under time pressure.

Every inspection is frozen

A check-in report still says what it said on the day, however many times you walk the property afterwards. Nothing later can quietly rewrite what was recorded at the start.

What changed, which is the argument

Run the comparison and you get one document listing only what is different between the check-in and the latest inspection, worse first. That is what a deposit conversation is actually about.

Reports that are honest about themselves

A line carried over from the last inspection rather than re-checked says so. Nothing ungraded is counted as better or worse. Meter readings carry the date the dial was read.

As many properties as you let

Each one keeps its own rooms, meters, tenancies and history, so a report can never mix two of them up and a tenant is only ever compared against their own check-in.

Reads a receipt for you

Photograph a receipt and the price and date are read off it on your device. Nothing is uploaded to do it.

Offline and private

No account and no cloud. Your items, photographs and your tenants' details stay on the phone unless you deliberately export them, and backups can be encrypted with a password only you hold.

Inventory+ is a record-keeping tool. It is not a valuation service and not legal advice, and it is not affiliated with any insurer, letting agent or deposit scheme. Values are the ones you enter.
