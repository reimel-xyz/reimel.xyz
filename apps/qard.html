Qard

Loan tracker · Repayment ledger · Qard hasan

Money you have lent, and money you owe, kept straight without a spreadsheet or an awkward conversation. Every repayment is recorded, so both sides can see exactly where a loan stands.

Privacy policy

Lending between people, recorded properly

Both directions, one list

Lent and borrowed sit together, with a running total for each, so you can see at a glance what is owed to you and what you owe.

A repayment ledger

Log each repayment as it happens and the outstanding balance follows. No arithmetic in your head, and no argument later about what has already been paid.

Reminders, and overdue flagged

Set a due date and get reminded before it. Anything late stays visible instead of quietly dropping down the list. A loan can also be marked as repayable when able, with no date at all.

Interest-free by design

Qard hasan means a loan with no interest, and the app has nowhere to put any. It records what was lent and what came back, nothing more.

Zakat export

Outstanding loans matter for zakat, so the figures can be handed to Muslim Budget or read straight off the summary.

Offline and private

No account and no cloud: names, amounts and dates stay on your device. No analytics, no tracking, no ads.

Qard is a record-keeping tool. It is not a lender, does not provide or arrange credit, and moves no money: it only tracks loans you have agreed yourself.
