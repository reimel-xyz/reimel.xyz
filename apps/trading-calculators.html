Trading Calculators

Position size · Risk and reward · Prop firm tracker

The arithmetic a trader does before every trade: how many contracts or lots, what the risk to reward really is, and how much drawdown buffer is left. Four calculators are free, with no ads.

Before every trade, the numbers

Position size for futures

Your balance, the risk you allow and your stop in points or ticks give the contracts to trade, and the risk you actually carry once the count is rounded down. Thirty-five contracts are built in, plus a custom point value.

And for forex

Fourteen pairs plus a custom one and eight account currencies, with lots sized from your stop in pips. Cross pairs use one rate you type from your platform, so no price feed is needed.

Risk and reward

Long or short: risk points, reward points, the ratio and the expected return, from your risk amount, entry, stop and target.

Tools

Compounding to a daily target, the gain needed to recover from a drawdown, the ticks to cover a round-trip commission, and the margin an FX position needs.

Stock profit and average down

Profit and loss on a multi-leg position at any target price, and how many shares to buy to lower your break-even or reach an average price you name.

Pro: the prop firm tracker

Evaluation and funded accounts with the drawdown floor, the daily loss limit left and progress to target, a pre-trade check, and a daily log. Pro is an optional subscription, monthly or yearly, cancelled any time in Google Play.

Your data stays on your device

No account, no sign-in, no analytics, no ads, no tracking. Accounts and trades are stored on your phone and never sent anywhere.

Independent

Not affiliated with, endorsed by or connected to any proprietary trading firm, broker or exchange.

Trading Calculators is a personal calculation tool. It does not provide financial advice, trading signals or investment recommendations, does not place trades and does not connect to any broker. All calculations are estimates from the inputs you provide, and your firm's own dashboard is the authority on its rules. Trading carries substantial risk of loss.
