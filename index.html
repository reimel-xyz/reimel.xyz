About Reimel

Reimel Ltd is a private limited company registered in England and Wales. We design and build small, privacy-respecting applications for everyday use, from utilities and calculators to reference and companion apps. Your data stays on your own device: our apps have no user accounts, no cloud sync and no analytics, and each one has its own privacy policy describing exactly what it does and does not do.
