Crew+: Deleting Your Account

Effective date: 27 October 2026
App: Crew+ (rota, clock in and time off for a business and its staff): the Android app, and the web clock-in at reimel.xyz/crew
Developer: Reimel Ltd (company no. 14523345, England and Wales) · company details

You can delete your Crew+ account yourself at any time, without asking your employer. Your login and the details that came with it are deleted. Your work and pay records stay with your employer for the periods the law sets, because they are its records too.

Delete it in the app

  1. Open Crew+ and sign in.
  2. Tap your picture at the top of Home, then Delete my account. On the web clock-in, Delete my account is at the bottom of Home. If your login isn’t on a team (you never joined one, or you have left), tap Delete this login on the screen you see after signing in.
  3. Read what is deleted and what is kept, enter your password, and tap Delete account.

A clock in saved on your phone with no signal is sent first, and the account isn’t deleted until it has gone.

If you can’t sign in

  1. Email support@reimel.xyz from the address you sign in with, with the subject “Crew+ account deletion”.
  2. Tell us the name of the business you work for, or worked for.
  3. We delete the account within 30 days and confirm by email.

What is deleted

  • Your login: the email and password you sign in with.
  • The phone number, email address and photo on your profile.
  • Each phone’s notification address, and the notifications kept for you.
  • The days you can’t work, with their notes.
  • An open shift you asked for that nobody has decided yet, and any invite code not yet used.

What your employer keeps, and for how long

  • Your name, role, job title and the dates you started and left.
  • Your rota and its history, your clock events and any changes to them, your time off, and shifts you gave up.
  • Your contracts and signatures, and documents such as payslips.
  • What the owner alone keeps: your pay rate, your date of birth and your right to work record.

Pay records are kept for six years, as minimum wage rules ask. Right to work copies are kept while you work there and for 2 years after you leave, as the right to work rules ask. Other work records stay until your employer removes them. Backup copies made before the deletion still hold your old details until each copy is removed, after 30 days. You stay on your employer’s team list without a login, and the owner and managers are told that you deleted your account. If you work there again, the owner can invite you, and a new login joins the same records.

Owners

A business’s records hang on its owner’s account, so an owner has two ways:

  • Keep the business going: in the app, make someone else the owner (Team, their page, Role), then change your own role. You can then delete your account like anyone, as above.
  • Close the business: in the app, Settings, Delete my account, then Close the company, which sends us the request (or email support@reimel.xyz from the address you sign in with). We send you a copy of the business’s records, then delete the business, everything in it and its staff’s logins within 30 days, and confirm by email. Keep what the law asks you to keep first, such as pay records for six years.
