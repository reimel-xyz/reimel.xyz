Reimel Social: Deleting Your Data

Effective date: 17 October 2026
Tool: Reimel Social (internal publishing tool, not available to the public)
Operator: Reimel Ltd (company no. 14523345, England and Wales) · company details

Reimel Social is the tool Reimel Ltd uses to publish to its own Facebook Page and Instagram account. Nobody else can sign in to it, so there is no account of yours to remove. The only data about you it can touch is a comment you left on one of our posts. The full picture is in our privacy policy.

What we may hold

  • The identifying number of a comment you left on one of our posts (never its text or your username).
  • A message in our team’s private Telegram chat showing your comment’s text, your public username and the time.

How to have it deleted

  1. Email support@reimel.xyz with the subject “Reimel Social data deletion”.
  2. Tell us your Facebook or Instagram username and, if you can, a link to the post you commented on.
  3. We delete everything we hold about you within 30 days and confirm by email.

Deleting the comment itself

You can delete your comment on Facebook or Instagram yourself at any time. That removes it at the source, and the tool can no longer read it. If you would rather we remove it from our post, say so in your email.

Contact
