Crew+ Privacy Notice for Staff

Effective date: 28 October 2026
App: Crew+ (rota, clock in and time off for a business and its staff): the Android app, and the web clock-in at reimel.xyz/crew
Publisher: Reimel Ltd (company no. 14523345, England and Wales) · company details

Your employer uses Crew+ to plan the rota, record when you clock in and out, and handle time off. This notice explains, plainly, what is kept about you, who holds it, where it is stored, how long it is kept and what you can ask for. Unlike our other apps, Crew+ keeps its records on a server, so that you, your managers and the owner of the business all see the same rota and the same hours.

Who holds your data

  • Your employer, the business that invited you to Crew+, decides what is recorded about you and for how long. In data protection law it is the controller, and it is the one to ask first about your records.
  • Reimel Ltd runs Crew+ and stores the records for your employer, as its processor, under an agreement with it. We use your data only to run Crew+ for your employer: never for advertising, never for analytics or tracking. We never sell it, and it reaches only the services named below, which carry it for us.

What is kept about you

  • You: your name, the email you sign in with, your role (staff, supervisor or owner), your job title, the dates you started and left, and how your holiday is worked out. Your password is kept only as a one-way scramble by the login service; nobody can read it, us included.
  • Your rota: your shifts, and a history of every change to them (when each was published, changed or cancelled, how much notice that gave, and who did it); the open shifts you ask for; the shifts you ask to give up and any reason you give; the days you can’t work and any note with them (a manager can add these too); and a mark when a shift was not worked.
  • Your clock events: each clock in, break and finish, with its time; how you clocked in (tapping the sign, scanning its code, or with no scan); the site; whether it was saved on a phone with no signal and sent later, and how far the phone’s clock was from the server’s; any change a manager makes to your times, with the reason and who made it; and which warnings a manager has checked.
  • Where your phone was, as an answer only: see Location below.
  • Time off: every kind (holiday, sickness, maternity, paternity and unpaid leave), its dates, any note, the answer and who gave it, and your holiday allowance and balance. A sick day is health information: your managers and the owner see it, other staff never do.
  • Pay: the hours you worked, and whether you are paid by the hour or the day. Your managers see your hours but never what you are paid.
  • Documents: any contract you sign in Crew+, with your signature, your typed name and the time you signed; and payslips or other documents your employer adds for you, which only you and the owner can open.
  • Contact details and a photo, if your employer adds them: a phone number and email address, so a manager can reach you about a shift, and a photo.
  • Kept by the owner alone: your pay rate; your date of birth (to check you are paid at least the legal minimum for your age, and to remind the owner of your birthday by its day and month; ask them to leave you out if you would rather not); whether the apprentice rate applies to you; and the record of your right to work check (the kind of permission you have and its dates), with copies of the documents or the online check you showed.
  • Your phone: a notification address for each phone you sign in on, so Crew+ can send you messages about your shifts and time off (see who else handles it). It is removed when you sign out, when you delete your account, or after 60 days unused. From the Android app, also which phone each clock in, break and finish came from: its model, and a code made from the phone’s own ID for this app, scrambled so it is not the ID itself. A manager sees a clock in from a new phone, or one phone used for two people. Nothing else is read from your phone, and the web clock-in sends neither.

Location

Crew+ reads your phone’s location once when you clock in and once when you finish, and at no other time: never during the shift, never in the background, never when the app or page is closed. Your phone compares that reading with the position of your workplace, and only the answer is sent: at the site, away from the site or not available. The Android app also says whether the phone’s location looked faked; the web clock-in cannot tell. The position itself is never sent and never kept.

Your employer uses the answer to know that time was recorded at work, and a manager checks any clock in or finish that was not confirmed at the site. If you block location, the web clock-in cannot clock you in or finish your shift, and nothing is recorded; it tells you how to allow it.

The web clock-in (for iPhones and other browsers)

The page at reimel.xyz/crew uses the same login and the same server as the app. In your browser it keeps:

  • your sign-in, and a copy of your name, your email, your workplaces and your own clock events from the last eight days, so the page opens with no signal. Signing out removes both;
  • any clock event made with no signal, until it has been sent;
  • the page itself and its two code libraries, so it opens with no signal.

The camera is used only while you scan the sign at work. The picture is read on your phone and is never kept or sent; the same goes for a photo of the sign if you take one instead. The page loads nothing from any other website: its files, including the two libraries, come from reimel.xyz, and it talks only to the Crew+ server.

Why it is kept

To plan the rota; to check that clocking in happens at work and that the times are honest (the location answer, a phone clock that is wrong, a clock in sent late); to pay you for the hours you worked; to handle your time off; to send you reminders about your shifts and tell your managers about a late start or a shift still open; and to keep the records the law asks an employer to keep (pay and minimum wage, holiday, working time and right to work). For most of this your employer relies on your employment contract and its legal duties as an employer; for the location answer and the clocking checks, on its legitimate interest in knowing that time is recorded honestly at work, which is why location is read only at those two moments and only the answer is kept.

Who can see it

  • You see most of your own records on your own screens (see your rights for the rest).
  • Your managers (the supervisors over you) see your rota, clock times and their warnings, time off with its notes, the reasons you give, and your contact details and photo, so they can run a shift. They never see your pay rate, your date of birth or your right to work record.
  • The owner sees everything above for the whole business.
  • Other staff: Crew+ shows them none of your records, apart from the first name of whoever gave up a shift that is now open to others. No other business using Crew+ can reach your records: the server keeps each business walled off.
  • Reimel Ltd can reach everything on the server to run it: to send notifications, to keep the backup below, to set the business’s plan and to fix faults. We look at records only for those reasons or when your employer asks.

Where it is stored, and who else handles it

  • Supabase keeps the database, the logins and the files (signatures, documents, photos) on Amazon Web Services servers in Ireland, in the EU. UK data protection law treats the EU as giving the same protection as the UK. Supabase also sends the login emails (confirming an account, a code for a forgotten password) and keeps short-lived technical logs of each request, such as the IP address, to run and protect the service. supabase.com/privacy.
  • Google Firebase Cloud Messaging carries the notifications the Android app shows. Their words pass through Google on the way to the phone: a new rota or a shift reminder for you, and for your managers messages such as “Alex is off sick today”, “Alex can’t do Mon 19 Oct, 09:00 to 17:00”, a late start, “Alex deleted their account” or, for the owner, the day a right to work permission ends. The reason you give for not doing a shift is never in these messages: your managers read it in the app. Messages about a named person’s time off, sickness, lateness, still being clocked in, a shift they can’t do, right to work or a deleted account are hidden on a locked phone. The server keeps the words of each message for 30 days. policies.google.com/privacy.
  • A nightly backup: each night Reimel Ltd copies the database (not the files, and not the logins or passwords) to its own computer in the UK, where each copy is kept for 30 days. Its own backups then carry that copy to its Microsoft OneDrive storage and to its own phone. The copy leaves out sign-in tokens, notification addresses, message words and invite codes.
  • GitHub hosts reimel.xyz, including the web clock-in page. Like any web host it receives the usual details of each request for a page or file (such as the IP address and the browser), but never your sign-in or your records. GitHub’s privacy statement.

All of it travels encrypted (HTTPS).

How long it is kept

Until your employer removes it: nothing that counts as a work or pay record is deleted by itself, because a record removed too early is one the law may still ask your employer to have. Minimum wage rules ask employers to keep pay records for six years. Right to work copies are kept while you work there and for 2 years after you leave, as the right to work rules ask. Once a year Crew+ lists for the owner the records it no longer needs (such as right to work copies past that date, and a leaver’s phone number, email and photo), and the owner confirms each removal.

Removed by themselves: the words of a notification after 30 days, a phone’s notification address after 60 days unused, and each backup copy after 30 days.

Deleting your account

You can delete your Crew+ account yourself at any time, without asking your employer.

  • Android app: tap your picture at the top of Home, then Delete my account.
  • Web clock-in: Delete my account at the bottom of Home.
  • A login that isn’t on a team (you never joined one, or you have left): sign in, then Delete this login.
  • If you can’t sign in: email support@reimel.xyz from the address you sign in with. We delete the account within 30 days and confirm by email.

You enter your password again to confirm. A clock in saved on your phone with no signal is sent first, and the account isn’t deleted until it has gone.

Deleted at once: your login (the email and password you sign in with), the phone number, email address and photo on your profile, each phone’s notification address and the notifications kept for you, the days you can’t work, an open shift you asked for that nobody has decided yet, and any invite code not yet used.

Kept by your employer: your work and pay records, for the periods in How long it is kept: your name, role, job title and dates, your rota and its history, your clock events and any changes to them, your time off, shifts you gave up, your contracts and signatures, your documents, and what the owner alone keeps (your pay rate, date of birth and right to work record). You stay on your employer’s team list without a login, and the owner and managers are told that you deleted your account. Backup copies made before the deletion still hold your old details until each copy is removed, after 30 days.

If you work there again, the owner can invite you, and a new login joins the same records.

A business’s records hang on its owner’s account, so an owner has two ways. To keep the business going, make someone else the owner (Team, their page, Role), change your own role, then delete your account like anyone. To close the business, use Delete my account in Settings, then Close the company, which sends us the request: we send you a copy of the business’s records, then delete the business, everything in it and its staff’s logins within 30 days. Keep what the law asks you to keep first.

Your rights

  • See your data: most of what Crew+ holds about you is on your own screens, and in the Android app Download my data (tap your picture at the top of Home) saves a copy of your own recent records. The owner can see everything, including your pay rate, date of birth, right to work record, the history of changes to your shifts and older records: ask your employer for a copy of anything not on your screens.
  • Correct it: ask your employer to correct anything that is wrong.
  • Delete your account: yourself, in the app or the web clock-in (see Deleting your account).
  • Delete other records or object: ask your employer. Some records must be kept for the periods the law sets, even after you leave.
  • Complain: if you are unhappy with how your data is handled, you can complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office, ico.org.uk.

We pass any request about your records to your employer, since it decides; we help it answer.

Security

Each business’s records are walled off from every other business on the server. Passwords are never stored readable and the connection is always encrypted. If something ever went wrong with your data, we would tell your employer without undue delay, so it can decide whether to report it to the Information Commissioner (within 72 hours, where the law asks) and to tell the staff affected.

Children

Crew+ is for people at work. Staff under 18 may use it; their date of birth is how the owner checks they are paid the right minimum for their age.
