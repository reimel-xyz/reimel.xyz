Reimel Social Privacy Policy

Effective date: 17 October 2026
Tool: Reimel Social (internal publishing tool, not available to the public)
Operator: Reimel Ltd (company no. 14523345, England and Wales) · company details

What Reimel Social is

Reimel Social is a tool Reimel Ltd uses to publish its own posts to the Reimel Apps Facebook Page and the @reimelapps Instagram account, and to read the figures and comments on those posts. Nobody outside Reimel Ltd can sign in to it or connect an account to it. It publishes only after a person at Reimel Ltd approves each post.

What it handles

  • Our own content: the videos, images and captions we publish.
  • Figures about our posts and accounts that Meta reports, such as views, reach, likes, shares, saves and follower counts. These are totals and do not identify anyone.
  • Comments on our posts. When someone comments on a Reimel Apps post, the tool reads the comment’s text, the commenter’s public username and the time, so a member of our team can read it and reply in the Facebook or Instagram app. The tool itself never replies, likes, follows or sends messages.

Where it goes, and what is kept

  • The tool runs on Reimel Ltd’s own computer in the United Kingdom, and talks to Meta through Meta’s official API.
  • New comments (text, username and time) are shown to our team in a private Telegram chat, which only our team can read. Telegram is the messaging service that delivers them.
  • The tool stores no comment text and no usernames. It keeps only the identifying number of each comment it has already shown us, so it does not show the same one twice, and the daily totals of the figures above. These records are included in our own backups.

What it is never used for

No advertising, no profiling, no selling or sharing with anyone else, and no AI. We do not contact anyone from this tool.

Why we may use it (UK GDPR)

Our legitimate interests: running our own social media accounts and answering the people who comment on our posts.

How long

The comment numbers and figure totals are kept for as long as we run these accounts. A comment shown in our team chat stays there as a message until we delete it, and we delete it on request.

Your choices and rights

  • You can delete your own comment on Facebook or Instagram at any time. Once it is deleted there, the tool can no longer read it.
  • You can ask us for a copy of anything we hold about you, or ask us to delete it. How to do that is on our data deletion page.
  • You can complain to the UK Information Commissioner’s Office (ico.org.uk).

What you post on Facebook and Instagram is also covered by Meta’s own privacy policy.

Changes

Any updates to this policy will be posted on this page.
